# Soccer Analyst Work
## Analysis of goal trend
This project will look at goal information from the top-level men's soccer teams in the UK, Italy and Spain. These are Premier league, Seria A and La liga, respectively. We will look at scoring trends of the last five years and compare among the three leagues.
